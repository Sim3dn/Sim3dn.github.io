About Me

I’m someone who genuinely enjoys exploring new tools and pushing creative boundaries to get the best results, even when working within tight constraints. I get a real kick out of diving into new software and experimenting with different techniques. Over time, I’ve built up a pretty broad skill set across design—modelling, texturing, lighting, rigging, and animation—and I tend to adapt depending on what the project needs most.

In past roles, I’ve often found ways to speed up workflows, whether by scripting or tweaking software to fit the task better—sometimes cutting down what would’ve been a week’s work into just a few days. I like problem-solving like that.

I’ll be honest—communication hasn’t always been my strongest area, but I’ve been working on it. I try to let the quality of my work speak for me, and I’m always pushing to do better, both creatively and professionally.
